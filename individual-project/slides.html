🔍 Key Weaknesses

  • Size distortion: Russia/Canada dominate; Singapore/Luxembourg invisible
  • Single variable: Cannot explore why countries are happy (GDP, health, freedom)
  • Weak comparison: Color differences between adjacent countries are hard to judge

✨ Three Redesign Decisions

1
Scatter plot over map — Eliminates area distortion; every country gets equal visual weight regardless of land mass.
2
Multi-variable encoding — X = switchable predictor (GDP/Social/LifeExp/Freedom), Y = Happiness, Size = Population, Color = Region.
3
Animated metric switching — Smooth D3 transitions let viewers ask "What if I compare happiness against freedom instead of GDP?"

📈 What Improved

  • Precise comparison: Aligned axes make Finland vs. Denmark easy to compare
  • Relationship discovery: Color reveals that Western Europe clusters high on both GDP and happiness, while some high-GDP Gulf states do not
  • Outlier detection: Countries above/below the trend line are immediately visible
