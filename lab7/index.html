❓ Temporal Network Questions & Findings

QuestionObservation
1How does overall connectivity change across 60 days? The network maintains a stable core of 6–8 persistent relationships that appear on most days. Daily link count fluctuates between 8–15, with spikes around Day 20 and Day 45 when cross-regional transactions increase. The animation reveals a dynamic but non-chaotic structure with an underlying stable backbone.
2Which companies become more central or active over time? Apex Textiles (c01) and Delta Energy (c04) consistently show the largest node sizes, indicating sustained high transaction volume. BlueRiver Logistics and Keystone Transport also remain prominent. Smaller companies like Lunar Software and Jade Pharma stay relatively small unless involved in rare large transactions.
3Are there periods with separate clusters? Yes. On Days 8–12, the force layout visibly separates into two or three spatial clusters by region. This happens when cross-regional transactions drop and only intra-regional links remain. Asia (red) and North America (blue) often drift apart, while Europe (green) sometimes bridges them.
4Which relationships appear, disappear, or strengthen? Persistent pairs (Apex–BlueRiver, Cedar–Horizon, Delta–Keystone) recur frequently. Some like IronWorks–Lunar appear only sporadically. Link width encodes transaction amount, so thicker lines indicate stronger commercial activity. Apex–Goldline emerges strongly around Day 30 then fades.
5Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time? The network shows waves rather than a steady trend. Days 20–35 show increased cross-regional links (more mixed-color connections). Days 45–60 return to more regional clustering. Cross-regional commerce is episodic, possibly reflecting scheduled shipping cycles.
